## Download and play

No Python needed. Pick the file for your computer from **Assets** below.

| Computer | File | How to start it |
|---|---|---|
| Windows 10 or 11 | `LearnTyping-windows.exe` | Double-click it. |
| Mac with Apple chip (M1 or newer) | `LearnTyping-macos-apple-silicon.zip` | Unzip, then double-click **Learn Typing**. |
| Older Mac with Intel chip | `LearnTyping-macos-intel.zip` | Unzip, then double-click **Learn Typing**. |
| Linux (64-bit) | `LearnTyping-linux.tar.gz` | Extract, then run `./LearnTyping`. |

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → **About This Mac**: "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple chip; "Processor: Intel" means Intel.

**The first time you open it, your computer may warn you.** The app isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft developer certificate, so they can't vouch for it:

- **Windows:** if you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac:** if you see "Apple could not verify…", click **Done**, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.

You only need to do this once. The first start can take a few seconds while the app unpacks.

Progress is saved on your computer, separately from the app, so downloading a newer version keeps everyone's stars.
